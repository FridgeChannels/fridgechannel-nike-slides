FAQ

Brand FAQs

Everything your growth, e-commerce, CRM, and operations teams might want to know before launching their first pilot.

A

Customer experience

What will customers actually receive?
Customers receive an NFC fridge magnet featuring your brand logo, either inside their order or included with it.
How will customers use it?
They attach it to their refrigerator, tap the magnet with their phone, complete simple challenges, earn points, and unlock rewards for their next purchase.
Do customers need to download an app?
No. The experience can be accessed directly in a mobile browser.
Do customers need to scan a QR code?
No. The core experience is based on NFC tap functionality.
Do customers need to register for an account?
Not necessarily. The pilot can be configured for low-barrier access, requiring customers to log in or link an account only when they need to use coupons, verify eligibility, or customize settings.
What happens if a customer taps multiple times?
Repeated taps can continue the rewards process, display current progress, show available rewards, or guide customers back to a call-to-action for their next purchase.
What happens if a customer shares the link?
Reward and coupon rules can include usage limits, expiration dates, customer eligibility requirements, or Shopify-side controls for coupon usage.
Is this experience too complicated for regular customers?
No. The customer flow stays brief: tap, play a game or answer a question, earn a reward, unlock it, and use the promo code. During the pilot we evaluate whether customers understand and complete the entire process.
B

NFC, devices & the physical magnet

Does NFC work properly on most phones?
This experience is designed for NFC-enabled iPhone and Android devices. FC verifies common device behavior during the pilot setup process.
Where should customers tap?
Brief tap instructions can be included in the magnet’s design or a packaging insert, depending on how much guidance you wish to provide.
What if a customer’s NFC isn’t working?
Alternate links or support channels can be provided if necessary, but the core process is designed around the NFC tap.
Is the magnet durable enough for kitchen use?
The magnet is designed as a long-term touchpoint in the kitchen rather than a single-use insert. Materials, surface finish, dimensions, and print quality are all verified prior to production.
Can we review the physical design before production?
Yes. You can review and approve the design draft before production begins.
Can the magnet be customized to match our brand style?
Yes. The front and back designs, color schemes, font styles, and mobile reward pages can all be tailored to match your brand’s official website.
Will it look like a cheap promotional item?
No. The goal is to make this a brand asset customers want to display on their refrigerator, rather than a one-time coupon card.
C

Rewards, coupons & reward packs

What types of rewards can customers unlock?
Rewards can include coupons, reward packs, free samples, bundle deals, restock offers, survey rewards, or incentives for the next order. Any reward that can be distributed as a promo code is acceptable.
Can we use our own Shopify promo codes?
Yes. Depending on your selected plan and integration scope, coupon rules can be synchronized with your Shopify coupon settings.
Can we control the discount amount and usage restrictions?
Yes. You can define the coupon value, validity period, usage limits, eligibility criteria, and usage rules.
Can we set different rewards for different customer segments?
Yes. You can configure rewards by segment — such as new, active, repeat, returning, and VIP customers.
Can I prevent excessive discounts?
Yes. You can set safeguards including discount caps, validity periods, usage limits, and rules to prevent customers from stacking offers or abusing promotions.
Can rewards be gifts instead of discounts?
Yes. Any reward that can be distributed in the form of a promo code is acceptable.
Can rewards be changed after the challenge goes live?
No. Once a round of the challenge goes live, the reward rules cannot be adjusted.
D

Brand control & customization

How many brand elements can be incorporated into the mobile experience?
The mobile experience can include your brand’s color scheme, tone of voice, campaign visuals, reward copy, and product-specific information. Brand elements are incorporated into both the home screen and the in-game interfaces.
Can the campaign reflect our brand rather than feel like a generic game?
Yes. The challenge’s copy, visuals, question design, and reward descriptions are all designed around the visual elements of your official website.
Can we run seasonal campaigns?
Yes. Campaigns can be tailored to scenarios such as new product launches, holidays, restocking windows, gift-giving seasons, subscription promotions, or limited-edition products.
E

Dashboard, data & performance

What information can be viewed in the dashboard?
The dashboard displays active magnets, click-throughs, challenge participation, unlocked promo codes, redeemed promo codes, orders, attributed revenue, repeat purchases, and revenue per active magnet.
Can I see how offline interactions convert to revenue?
Yes. The conversion funnel links offline touchpoint activity to promo code redemptions, orders, and attributed revenue.
What is an active magnet?
An active magnet is a touchpoint that has generated at least one trackable interaction during the selected time period.
What is “attributed revenue per active magnet”?
It shows the amount of attributed revenue generated by magnets that actually produced interactions.
Can I compare different customer segments?
Yes, depending on your plan and settings. Segment-level metrics can show how different audiences respond to different offers.
Can we determine whether this increases the repeat purchase rate?
The pilot is designed to measure metrics such as repeat orders, promo code usage, restocking behavior, and revenue attributed to campaigns.
Is ROAS guaranteed?
No. ROAS estimates are planning assumptions and do not constitute guaranteed results. The pilot validates actual customer behavior before a large-scale rollout.
What are the most critical metrics for the pilot?
For the initial pilot: active magnets, click-through rate, repeat clicks, unlocked promo codes, redeemed promo codes, repeat orders, attributed revenue, and revenue per active magnet.
F

Setup, operations & responsibilities

How long does setup take?
Setup time depends on design draft approval, coupon rules, dashboard scope, Klaviyo audience segmentation, Shopify configuration, and the production schedule. The first step is to confirm the pilot scope and go-live checklist.
What information does FC need before launch?
Brand assets, the number of trial units, the target customer segment, reward rules, Shopify account or coupon settings, Klaviyo account or audience segmentation settings, shipping times, and success metrics.
What do we need to complete internally?
Confirm the campaign objectives, review the lead magnet and mobile experience, establish coupon or reward rules, provide Shopify and Klaviyo setup details, and coordinate shipping of the lead magnet.
Who adds the freebies to customer orders?
This depends on your fulfillment setup. Giveaways can be added to orders by your fulfillment team, a third-party logistics (3PL) provider, or your campaign operations partner.
Can we run a small-scale pilot first?
Yes. We recommend a small-scale pilot before scaling up, to validate click-through rates, reward conversion, and the impact on repeat purchases.
What pilot size do you recommend?
For the initial test, choose a sample large enough to observe the behavior of a representative customer group, yet small enough to keep costs and fulfillment complexity under control.
What happens after the pilot ends?
FC and your team jointly evaluate performance, identify the best-performing segments or reward schemes, and decide whether to scale up, adjust the program, renew for a second year, or conclude the project.
G

Pricing, plans & scaling

Which plan is best for us?
For brands with repeat purchase potential, “Retention Moat” can be an ideal starting point, as segment-based incentives directly drive restocking and retention.
What is included in the first-year setup fee?
The first year includes the initial batch of physical fridge magnets plus system setup and launch services. The exact scope depends on the selected package and confirmed pilot requirements.
Why is a second-year renewal required?
Renewal keeps the tap experience, reward tiers, dashboard, and campaign infrastructure fully operational after the first year.
Can I upgrade after the initial pilot ends?
Yes. You can start with a simpler plan and upgrade later as campaigns move from brand awareness toward segment-based LTV growth or retention analysis.
Can I run multiple campaigns using the same magnet?
In principle, yes. The same physical magnet can serve as an ongoing touchpoint for new reward rounds, seasonal promotions, surveys, and restocking campaigns.
How is this different from email or text messages?
Emails and texts compete for attention in crowded digital channels. A magnet creates a physical reminder in the home that encourages customers to return when they’re in the kitchen or need to restock.
How is this different from a regular coupon insert?
Standard inserts are usually discarded after a single use. NFC magnets are designed to stay visible, generate repeat interactions, and link those interactions to measurable revenue.
