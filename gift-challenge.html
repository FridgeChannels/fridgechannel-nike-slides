What goal we're solving for

01.1 — Immediate win

Immediate, measurable win

Increase repeated orders and refine retention for every customer who already received a package.

Grow Household Active Client rate to 50%+.

Appendix — FAQ

Brand FAQs

Everything your growth, e-commerce, CRM, and operations teams might want to know before launching a first pilot.

Do customers need to download an app?
No. Customers simply tap the NFC fridge magnet to open your brand’s mobile rewards page directly in their browser.
Do customers need to scan a QR code?
No. The feature is activated by NFC touch, so customers do not need to open their camera or scan a QR code.
Is this feature supported on iPhone and Android devices?
Yes, this experience is designed for NFC-enabled iPhone and Android devices. During the pilot deployment, FC will verify that tap-to-activate works properly on common devices before the official launch.
What specific content do you need to provide?
Brand assets, logos, and color schemes; campaign rules; target audience demographics; terms for coupons or reward packs; Shopify account or coupon settings; Klaviyo account or audience segmentation settings; shipping times; and success-rate metrics.
What is FC responsible for?
FC prepares the brand magnet settings, mobile rewards experience, campaign configuration, coupon logic, dashboards, pilot checklists, and reporting structures.
How do customers redeem rewards?
Customers tap the “Magnet” button, complete the challenge, and unlock a coupon or reward pack. The system then redirects them back to your checkout, where they can apply the promo code on their next order.
How can I determine if the campaign is effective?
The dashboard tracks active magnets, click-throughs, challenge participation, unlocked promo codes, redeemed promo codes, repeat orders, attributed revenue, and revenue generated per active magnet.
Can rewards be customized for different customer segments?
Yes. Depending on your selected plan, you can assign offers to segments based on lifecycle stage — such as new, active, returning, repeat, and VIP customers.
What happens if a customer doesn’t tap?
This is exactly what the pilot is designed to measure. The initial pilot validates the magnet’s activation rate, click-through behavior, coupon usage, repurchase rate, and whether this channel is worth scaling.
What is the recommended next step?
Start with a limited-scope pilot: define the target audience, volume, reward rules, shipping windows, Shopify settings, Klaviyo segmentation, and success metrics. FC will prepare the pilot experience and dashboard before launch.
